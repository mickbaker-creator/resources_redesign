Discover the real cost of recruitment in 2025

Recruiting and retaining talent in Australia has never been more challenging. In a tightening labour market, small to medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) are under increasing pressure to stay competitive — not just in how they hire, but in how they invest in the process.

Our new 2025 Hiring Snapshot Report reveals the current state of SME recruitment across Australia, highlighting how hiring strategies, budgets and behaviours have shifted in response to economic and workforce changes.

From recruitment spend to staff turnover, and from digital hiring platforms to unconventional recruitment strategies — the findings paint a compelling picture of what hiring looks like in 2025.

  • What are SMEs really spending on recruitment each year?
  • Which hiring platforms are most commonly used, and which deliver the best ROI?
  • What's causing new hires to leave within their first month?
  • How are businesses dealing with persistent staff shortages?

You'll also discover how industry, business size and turnover influence hiring strategies, and how some employers are getting creative in a competitive job market.

Why it matters now

With operating costs rising and talent harder to find, the ability to hire well — and retain great people — is more important than ever. The 2025 Hiring Snapshot Report provides critical benchmarking data to help you assess how your hiring practices compare to others in your space.

Whether you're adjusting your recruitment budget, exploring new hiring channels, or working to improve early employee retention, this report arms you with the insights needed to make informed decisions.

What is the latest recruitment strategy?

The most effective recruitment strategies in 2025 are those embracing skills-based hiring, AI-powered screening, and improved candidate experience. Employers are shifting away from rigid qualifications and prioritising practical, transferable skills that align with their evolving business needs.

What is the biggest challenge in recruitment?

The biggest hurdle facing hiring managers today is the persistent skills gap. Many industries are experiencing a mismatch between available roles and the capabilities of job seekers — compounded by fierce competition and stretched recruitment budgets, particularly for SMEs.
