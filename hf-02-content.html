Session Overview

The hidden cost of employment

Do you know what employment is really costing your business? Most employers don't. Beyond salary, a hidden layer of compliance, admin, and overhead drives costs significantly higher — often across fragmented systems that don't work together. A $100k salary can quickly become $150k+ in real cost, and that gap is where time, money, and momentum are lost.

A new model for employment

In this session, we'll unpack the true cost of employment — where it comes from, why it's increasing, and how it's holding businesses back. Then, we'll show you what comes next.

You'll get an exclusive first look at HeroForce — a new model for employment that removes the administrative and compliance burden entirely, bringing hiring, payroll, and workforce management into a single, AI-powered system that runs employment for you.

Who should attend

Employment is likely your biggest cost but one of the least optimised parts of your business, and those who rethink it will reduce overheads, hire faster, and scale with less risk. This session will give you a clear view of what's broken, and exclusive access to what's replacing it.
